Data Protection

Privacy-Ready Customer Data Management

Teboa is designed with customer data protection in mind for modern online businesses handling names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, support messages, and order history every day. That information needs to be handled responsibly, kept secure, and used in ways that respect customer privacy. Teboa helps centralise customer information inside a structured workspace so teams are not passing sensitive data around through scattered tools and informal processes. By reducing fragmentation, businesses get better control over who can access data, how customer records are used, and how operational workflows stay aligned with privacy expectations. Strong privacy practices are not only a legal issue. They are part of building trust with customers who expect their data to be handled carefully. Teboa supports that standard while still giving stores the automation, support visibility, and business context they need to run effectively.
