Why Teboa exists

Shopify already handles commerce well. The operational problem begins when support, customer follow-up, performance, and workflows become scattered across separate apps and inboxes. Teboa brings that work into one clearer operating system.

What makes Teboa useful

AI support routing

Understand customer intent and separate routine questions from issues needing human attention.

Shared store intelligence

See orders, customers, revenue, support, and automation activity with consistent context.

Behavior-aware retention

Guide follow-up using customer value, activity, and purchase behavior.

Plug-and-play operations

Reduce setup work with practical workflows and one place to monitor activity.
